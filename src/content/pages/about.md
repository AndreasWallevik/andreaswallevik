title: About Andreas
description: About Andreas Wallevik, a Norwegian songwriter, music producer and multidisciplinary creative.
---

I’m Andreas Wallevik, a songwriter, music producer and creative from Hardanger, now based in Tønsberg, Norway.

My work moves between electronic music, ambient sounds, pop and hip-hop. I’ve worked across songwriting, vocals, production, sound design and video, and I’m interested in what happens when sound and image find a shared rhythm.

My projects have taken different forms: music made under my own name, collaborations, and production music for visual use. That includes work for Stereo Royal / Upright Music and the independently released sync album *Dark Ocean Waves*.

I also have a background in software development, which occasionally finds its way into my creative process.
